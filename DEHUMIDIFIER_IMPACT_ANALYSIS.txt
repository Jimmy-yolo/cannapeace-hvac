================================================================================
ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
================================================================================

WHY IS THE DEHUMIDIFIER COUNTERPRODUCTIVE?

1. COOLING EFFECT:
   The dehumidifier removes heat from the room while attempting to dehumidify.
   This 0.62°C drop triggers the HVAC heating mode or forces the cooling
   system to reduce output, disrupting the VPD control algorithm.

2. INEFFECTIVE MOISTURE REMOVAL:
   Despite running continuously, the dehumidifier increased humidity by 0.43%.
   Possible reasons:
   - Unit is undersized for the space
   - Condensate is not being removed properly (re-evaporating)
   - Airflow pattern conflicts with HVAC system
   - Unit efficiency is poor in Bangkok's climate

3. HVAC SYSTEM CONFLICT:
   The Altaqua system has sophisticated VPD control that:
   - Monitors both temperature and humidity
   - Adjusts both cooling and dehumidification automatically
   - The external dehumidifier interferes with this control loop
   - HVAC compensates for unwanted cooling, increasing energy use

4. VPD CONTROL CONFUSION:
   The improved VPD reading is misleading:
   - It's achieved through cooling (reducing SVP), not dehumidification
   - The HVAC thinks VPD is "better" so it backs off humidity control
   - This actually makes moisture removal LESS effective
   - You're essentially cooling to improve VPD, which wastes energy
