# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
## Condenser Evaporative Pre-Cooler Feasibility - GAS-20D Unit

**Date:** June 8, 2026
**Recommendation:** DO NOT INSTALL (at this time)
**Confidence:** MODERATE (limited by data gaps)

---

## THE BOTTOM LINE

**Installing a condenser evaporative pre-cooler is NOT economically justified** based on analysis of 1.55 million data points from April-May 2026.

**Expected outcome:** NEGATIVE annual returns (-6,000 to -26,000 THB/year losses)
**Capital at risk:** 150,000 THB installation cost
**Simple payback:** INFINITE (ongoing costs exceed savings)

---

## THREE KEY REASONS

### 1. NO THERMAL STRESS DETECTED
- Only 0.6-0.7% of operating time shows high condensing temperatures (>55°C)
- System operates in healthy temperature range 99%+ of time
- No evidence condenser is heat-limited

### 2. ADEQUATE CAPACITY
- Peak afternoon runtime: only 44-50% (substantial headroom)
- System not capacity-limited during hottest conditions
- Pre-cooler would reduce runtime minimally (45% → 42%)

### 3. POOR ECONOMICS
**Energy savings:** ~7,000 kWh/year = 31,400 THB/year
**Operating costs:** Water (22,500 THB) + Maintenance (15,000 THB) = 37,500 THB/year
**NET RESULT:** -6,100 THB/year (loses money)

Even in best-case scenario, payback exceeds 7 years.

---

## CRITICAL FINDING: LOAD/CLIMATE MISMATCH

**Worst possible timing:**
- Peak cooling loads occur at NIGHT (lights ON 21:00-09:00)
- Bangkok nighttime humidity: 85-95% RH
- Wet bulb depression at night: only 2-3°C (very poor evaporative potential)

**Pre-cooler would work best during afternoon when:**
- System is lightly loaded (lights OFF, low cooling demand)
- Benefit is wasted on low-load conditions

**Result:** Pre-cooler effectiveness reduced by ~50% compared to standard installation.

### What We Have (GOOD)
- 36 days of 1-second interval data

### What's MISSING (CRITICAL GAPS)

- ❌ No outdoor humidity → Can't calculate wet bulb depression
- ❌ No compressor power → Can't confirm energy savings
- ❌ No discharge line temp → Can't assess system health

**Impact:** All economic estimates have ±50% or higher uncertainty.

**Install:**

**This approach:**
- Costs 1/3 as much as pre-cooler
- Eliminates financial risk of wrong decision
- Provides ongoing operational visibility
- Supports future optimization regardless of pre-cooler decision

## WHEN WOULD PRE-COOLER BE JUSTIFIED?

Pre-cooler installation would make sense if future data shows:

1. **Actual compressor power >25 kW** (vs assumed 18 kW)
2. **Frequent thermal stress** (>10% of time above 55°C condensing)
3. **High peak runtime** (>80% during afternoon)
4. **Excessive condensing approach** (>20°C above outdoor temp)
5. **Need for capacity expansion** (pre-cooler cheaper than new equipment)

**Current data shows NONE of these conditions exist.**

## ALTERNATIVE IMPROVEMENTS (LOWER COST/RISK)

### Option 1: Clean and Optimize Condenser (5,000-15,000 THB)
- Clean coils thoroughly
- Verify fan operation
- Remove airflow restrictions
- **Benefit:** 2-5% efficiency gain, one-time cost

### Option 2: Control Optimization (0-20,000 THB)
- Raise setpoint 1°C (if plant-acceptable)
- Optimize DEHUM sequence
- Night setback during cool periods
- **Benefit:** 3-10% energy reduction, minimal cost

### Option 3: Do Nothing
- System shows adequate performance
- No thermal stress or capacity issues
- Monitor for 6-12 months
- Revisit if conditions change
- **Cost:** 0 THB
