## Why Is This Different from Previous Analysis?

The previous comprehensive analysis (DEHUMIDIFIER_IMPACT_ANALYSIS_REPORT.md) concluded to turn off dehumidifiers based on June 8-9 data. This new real-world test confirms:

**Previous Analysis:** Predicted minimal benefit, high cost
**Actual Result:** Confirmed - 927W savings, only 0.5% humidity increase

**Why did HVAC load increase after turnoff?**
- Dehumidifiers WERE helping remove moisture
- HVAC now does ALL the dehumidification work
- BUT HVAC is more efficient (heat pump vs resistive heating)
- Net result: 73W more HVAC, but 1000W less total

**The key insight:** HVAC can handle the load efficiently without supplemental dehumidifiers.

**Medium-term (next week):**

**Long-term (next month):**
- Calculate actual energy savings from utility bills
- Confirm no mold or moisture issues

### If Humidity Becomes Problematic:

**Before turning dehumidifiers back on, try:**
1. **Adjust watering schedule** - Move feeding to lights-off period (09:00-21:00)
2. **Improve air circulation** - Add/optimize fans
3. **Check HVAC maintenance** - Clean filters, check refrigerant
4. **Adjust VPD setpoint** - Fine-tune HVAC parameters

**Only turn dehumidifiers back on if:**
- Humidity consistently exceeds 62% for extended periods
- Mold or moisture issues develop
- Plant stress indicators appear
